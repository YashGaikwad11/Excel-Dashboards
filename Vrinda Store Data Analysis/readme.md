## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard created to analyze the annual sales performance of Vrinda Store for the year 2022. The dashboard provides valuable business insights related to sales trends, customer demographics, order status, and sales channels using data visualization techniques.

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales vs Orders
- Monthly sales and order trends were analyzed together.
- Highest sales were observed in March.
- Sales gradually declined towards the end of the year.

### 2. Gender-wise Sales Analysis
- Women contributed approximately **64%** of total sales.
- Men contributed approximately **36%** of total sales.

### 3. Order Status Analysis
- Around **92%** of orders were successfully delivered.
- Very few orders were cancelled, refunded, or returned.

### 4. Top Performing States
- Maharashtra recorded the highest sales.
- Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh were among the other top-performing states.

### 5. Age & Gender Analysis
- Adult women contributed the highest share of purchases.
- Teenagers and senior citizens contributed comparatively less.

### 6. Sales Channel Performance
- Amazon emerged as the leading sales channel.
- Myntra and Flipkart also contributed significantly to sales.
